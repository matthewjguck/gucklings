## How to Use Gucklings

### Send a Letter
1. Tap "Compose"
2. Write your message
3. Add stickers or signature (optional)
4. Choose who to send to
5. Tap "Send to Printer"
6. Letter prints at home! 🖨️

### View Letters Sent to You
1. Go to "Home" 
2. See letters from family members
3. Tap to read

### See Your Letters
1. Go to "Archive"
2. All your sent letters are saved

### Manage Your Profile
1. Go to "Profile"
2. Update display name or avatar
